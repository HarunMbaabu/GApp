FAQ

Questions before you move to self-hosted collaboration.

What is Mattermost?

Mattermost is an open-source collaboration platform for team messaging, channels, file sharing, and workplace communication.

Is Glo Talk the same as Mattermost?

No. Glo Talk is a professional service provider. We help businesses install, configure, secure, migrate to, and manage self-hosted Mattermost.

Do I need my own server?

You can use an existing server, request help choosing one, or ask us to guide hosting preparation during discovery.

Can you help us buy or configure a server?

Yes. We can recommend server requirements, help configure the environment, and prepare it for Mattermost deployment.

Can we use our own domain?

Yes. We configure your chosen domain and SSL certificate so your team can access a professional secure URL.

Can we migrate from Slack or Teams?

Yes. We provide migration guidance and planning. The exact approach depends on your current exports, channels, files, and user needs.

Is it secure?

Self-hosting gives you more control, and Glo Talk applies practical security hardening, HTTPS, admin guidance, and backup planning.

Do you provide training?

Yes. Packages include admin training, and optional staff training can be added for larger teams.

Do you offer monthly support?

Yes. Monthly maintenance, monitoring, updates, and dedicated support can be added after installation.

Can this work for schools or communities?

Yes. Schools, cohorts, NGOs, communities, and training institutions can use private Mattermost spaces for organized communication.
